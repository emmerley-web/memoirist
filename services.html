What I offer

Services

Three services, shaped around different stages of the writing process. All with the same goal: a book you're proud to put your name on.

01

Ghostwriting

A full cowriting experience, from first conversation to finished manuscript, built around capturing your voice.

We start with a proper consultation: what the book is about, who it's for, the tone and style you want. From there I put together an outline, and once that's agreed, we move into interview sessions.

The interviews are where the real work happens. Through open, unhurried conversation I draw out the experiences and details that make your story yours. Between sessions, I write. You read it, and we adjust.

When it's done, the manuscript is completely yours. Your name on it, your copyright, your royalties. Most clients find it sounds more like them than anything they'd written themselves.

  • Initial consultation and concept development
  • Detailed outline and narrative structure
  • Regular interview and story-extraction sessions
  • Full manuscript drafting (up to 50,000 words)
  • Multiple revision rounds
  • Publishing guidance (traditional, hybrid, or self-publishing)
  • 100% copyright and royalty retention for the client

02

Book Proposal Writing

For writers aiming at traditional publishing. A well-crafted proposal is what gets you in front of agents, and I'll help you write one.

Traditional publishing starts with a proposal. A good one is part business case, part writing sample. It needs to say what the book is, why it matters now, who will buy it, and why you are the right person to write it.

I work through each component with you: the overview, the sample chapters that show your voice, the market analysis that makes the commercial case.

  • Project overview and hook
  • Author biography and platform summary
  • Table of contents and chapter-by-chapter outline
  • Market analysis and comparable titles
  • Two to three polished sample chapters
  • Guidance on agent submission strategy
Enquire about proposals
03

Developmental Editing

Already have a draft? Developmental editing looks at the big picture. Structure, arc, pacing. Making the manuscript stronger without changing how you write.

This is for writers with a complete or near-complete draft who want an expert eye on what's working and what isn't. This is different from copyediting or proofreading. It works at the level of story structure, not surface errors.

You'll get a detailed editorial report: what's working, what needs attention, and how to address it. Then we work through revisions together.

  • Thorough manuscript read and analysis
  • Comprehensive written editorial report
  • Narrative arc and structure assessment
  • Voice, pacing, and chapter-level feedback
  • One to two revision support rounds
  • Final manuscript review
