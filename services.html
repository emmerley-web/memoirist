What I offer

Services

Three distinct services, one underlying commitment: producing work that is authentic, beautifully written, and entirely yours.

01

Ghostwriting

A full-service cowriting experience — from initial concept through to a polished, publishable manuscript — designed to capture your voice, your story, and your truth.

Ghostwriting is a deeply collaborative process. We begin with a detailed consultation to understand the big picture: concept, genre, key themes, tone, style, and target audience. From there, I develop a comprehensive outline before we move into in-depth interview sessions.

These interviews are the heart of the work. Through careful, open-ended questioning I draw out the experiences, emotions, and details that make your story singular. Between sessions, I draft — and you review, redirect, and refine.

The final manuscript is yours in every sense: your name, your copyright, your royalties. Many clients are also surprised to find that the book sounds more like them than anything they could have written alone.

  • Initial consultation and concept development
  • Detailed outline and narrative structure
  • Regular interview and story-extraction sessions
  • Full manuscript drafting (up to 50,000 words)
  • Multiple revision rounds
  • Publishing guidance (traditional, hybrid, or self-publishing)
  • 100% copyright and royalty retention for the client

02

Book Proposal Writing

For authors seeking traditional publishing representation — a compelling, agent-ready proposal that presents your book at its very best.

Traditional publishing starts with a proposal, and a great proposal is part business case, part writing sample. It needs to communicate what your book is, why it matters right now, who will buy it, and why you are the person to write it.

I work with you to craft each component carefully — from the high-level overview to the sample chapters that demonstrate your voice and the market analysis that demonstrates your book's commercial potential.

  • Project overview and hook
  • Author biography and platform summary
  • Table of contents and chapter-by-chapter outline
  • Market analysis and comparable titles
  • Two to three polished sample chapters
  • Guidance on agent submission strategy
Enquire about proposals
03

Developmental Editing

Already have a draft? Developmental editing works at the structural and narrative level — making your manuscript stronger without changing your voice.

This service is for writers who have produced a complete or near-complete draft and want expert guidance on what is working and what needs attention. Developmental editing is not copyediting or proofreading — it operates at the level of story, structure, and character.

You'll receive a detailed editorial report outlining the strengths of your manuscript, areas for development, and specific, actionable recommendations. We then work through revisions together until the manuscript reaches its potential.

  • Thorough manuscript read and analysis
  • Comprehensive written editorial report
  • Narrative arc and structure assessment
  • Voice, pacing, and chapter-level feedback
  • One to two revision support rounds
  • Final manuscript review
