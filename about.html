Her approach

Writing that listens before it speaks.

Emily Liao is a ghostwriter and memoir collaborator. She has ghostwritten, edited, and consulted on more than ten books with clients across four continents.

She's worked across a lot of ground: business founders, artists, people whose lives have crossed many borders. Stories that wouldn't exist without someone asking the right questions. She spends a long time in conversation with each client before she writes a word.

"Writing should be frictionless, accessible, and, above all, generous."

Clients often say the finished book sounds more like them than anything they'd have written themselves.

Philosophy

On storytelling
and craft

Emily approaches every project with the same conviction: no story is too small or too ordinary. What matters is specificity. The precise memory, the exact word, the kind of detail that makes a reader stop.

She specialises in memoir because the personal tends to be more universal than people expect. Good memoir does more than record what happened. It finds the meaning in it.

Working closely with someone on their story takes trust on both sides. Emily takes that seriously.
