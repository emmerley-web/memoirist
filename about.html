About

Emily Liao

Memoirist, ghostwriter, and book collaborator — helping remarkable people tell remarkable stories, from London to four continents.

Photo of Emily Liao

Her approach

Writing that listens before it speaks.

Emily Liao is a London-based ghostwriter and memoir collaborator. She has ghostwritten, edited, and consulted on more than ten memoirs and business books — working with clients across four continents whose stories span entrepreneurial achievement, artistic perseverance, and cross-cultural experience.

Her practice is built on a belief that remarkable stories exist in every life — and that the right questions, asked at the right moment, will always find them. She conducts extended interview sessions with each client before a word of draft writing is produced.

"Writing should be frictionless, accessible, and, above all, generous."

The result is writing that clients consistently describe as sounding more authentically like themselves than anything they could have produced alone.

Philosophy

On storytelling
and craft

Emily approaches every project with the conviction that no story is too small, too ordinary, or too niche. What matters is specificity — the precise memory, the telling detail, the exact word that makes a reader stop and say: yes, that's it exactly.

She specialises in memoir because she believes the personal is the universal. The best memoir writing doesn't just document a life — it illuminates something true about being human. That's what she's working towards, in every session, with every client.

The ghostwriting relationship requires trust, honesty, and patience — and Emily considers these as much a part of the craft as the writing itself.
