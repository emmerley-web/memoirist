About ghostwriting

Individuals who have compelling stories or expertise but lack the time, writing skills, or confidence to write a book themselves. A ghostwriter provides the structure, craft, and sustained dedication needed to develop raw ideas and lived experience into a polished, publishable manuscript. Entrepreneurs, executives, creatives, and people with extraordinary life stories are among the most common clients.
Beyond any direct revenue from sales, a well-crafted book is a powerful professional and personal asset. It can open doors to speaking engagements, consulting opportunities, and high-value clients. It builds credibility and authority in ways few other mediums can match. And, for many clients, it is simply a deeply personal milestone — a way of ensuring their story is told and preserved.
Entirely. Ghostwriting is a long-established, widely practised profession. Political memoirs, celebrity autobiographies, and business books are routinely produced with professional writing support. The idea, the story, and the voice remain the client's — the ghostwriter provides the craft and the labour to bring them into form. There is nothing deceptive about using a professional to help tell your story.
This is the central challenge of ghostwriting — and the thing Emily takes most seriously. Voice is developed through extensive listening: hours of interview sessions in which you speak freely and naturally, before any drafting begins. Many clients find the finished manuscript sounds more authentically like them than anything they've written themselves, precisely because the best ghostwriting draws out what's already there, rather than imposing something new.

The process

Timelines vary depending on project scope, complexity, and client availability. As a general guide, expect 6–12 months for a complete ghostwritten manuscript of up to 50,000 words. Shorter projects or book proposals can be delivered more quickly. We'll agree a clear timeline at the outset.
The final deliverable is a complete, polished book manuscript — ready for submission to a literary agent, a publisher, or for self-publishing. As part of the process, we'll also discuss the right publishing route for your book and I can provide guidance and referrals accordingly.
More than you might expect — but in an enjoyable way. The interviews and story-extraction sessions are where your book is born, and your participation is essential. After that, I take care of the drafting, but you'll review each section and give feedback. The process is designed to minimise the burden on your time while keeping you genuinely in control of the work.
Yes — all interview sessions and check-ins can be conducted remotely via video call. Emily is London-based and has worked with clients across four continents. Location is not a barrier to working together.

Copyright & ownership

You do — entirely. Copyright belongs exclusively to the client, as do all royalties from the sale of the book. This is made explicit in the written agreement before work begins. Your name goes on the cover; the work is yours in every legal and creative sense.
This is entirely your choice. Some clients choose to acknowledge their collaborator in the book's acknowledgements — others prefer complete confidentiality. Either is absolutely fine and we'll agree this in advance. Discretion is a core part of the professional relationship.

Publishing

Yes — as part of the ghostwriting engagement, we'll discuss the most appropriate publishing route for your book. Whether that's traditional publishing (requiring an agent and proposal), hybrid publishing, or self-publishing, I can provide guidance and, where relevant, warm referrals to trusted partners.
