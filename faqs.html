About ghostwriting

People who have a story worth telling but don't have the time, the writing background, or the confidence to do it alone. Business founders, executives, artists, people who've lived through something worth putting down. A ghostwriter brings the structure and the craft; you bring everything else.
Beyond the direct revenue from sales, a well-written book does a lot of other work. It can lead to speaking engagements, consulting enquiries, and clients who found you through the book. It builds a kind of credibility that's hard to manufacture any other way. For many people, though, it's simpler than that: they want their story on record while they still can tell it.
Yes. Ghostwriting has been around for as long as publishing. Political memoirs, celebrity autobiographies, business books: most are written with professional help. The story, the ideas, and the voice are the client's. The ghostwriter provides the craft and the time to get it done. There's nothing dishonest about that.
This is the hardest part of ghostwriting, and the one Emily spends the most time on. Voice comes from listening: hours of conversation in which you speak freely, before any writing starts. Most clients find the finished manuscript sounds more like them than anything they'd have written themselves. That's the point.

The process

Timelines vary depending on project scope, complexity, and client availability. As a general guide, expect 6–12 months for a complete ghostwritten manuscript of up to 50,000 words. Shorter projects or book proposals can be delivered more quickly. We'll agree a clear timeline at the outset.
A complete, polished book manuscript, ready for wherever you want to take it: a literary agent, a publisher, or self-publishing. We'll talk through the right route during the process, and I can help with next steps or point you to the right people.
More than you might think, but in a good way. The interview sessions are where the book actually comes from, and those need you. After that, I handle the writing, but you read each section and give feedback as we go. The process is built to take as little of your time as possible while keeping you in control of the work.
Yes. All sessions are done by video call. Emily has worked with clients across four continents. Location has never been a problem.

Copyright & ownership
