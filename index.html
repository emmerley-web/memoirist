Who this is for

People with a story
worth telling

Ghostwriting suits those with a story worth telling and a genuine reason to tell it. People who have the experience and the material, but not always the time, the writing background, or the confidence to do it justice on their own.

Clients include founders and executives who want their career on record, the pivots and hard lessons worth passing on. People writing a legacy memoir for family who will one day want to know where they came from. Survivors, artists, and advocates who have lived through something that deserves to be documented.

The common thread is not achievement or fame but having something genuine to say, and the willingness to say it honestly.

Your voice, not mine.

The manuscript will sound like you wrote every word. Before anything goes on the page, Emily spends hours in conversation with each client, learning how they speak, what they reach for, and what they tend to leave out, because those omissions are often where the real voice lives.

Your name on the cover.

Copyright and all royalties stay with you, set out clearly in a written agreement before any work begins. Emily takes no credit unless you choose to offer it.

Confidential by design.

Everything discussed in the process stays between you. All engagements include a written NDA, and discretion is treated as a professional requirement rather than an optional extra.
