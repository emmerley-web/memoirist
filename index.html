The process

How it works

Many clients have been sitting on their story for years. They know what they want to say, but haven't been able to make it happen alone. This is where it starts. Each project is different, but the shape tends to look like this.

01

Free consultation

We discuss your story, goals, timeline, and whether we're the right fit for each other.

02

Interviews & drafting

Regular sessions to draw out your story. Between sessions, I write. You read and give feedback.

03

Revise & deliver

Revision rounds until it's right. When it's done, the manuscript is entirely yours.

About Emily

Writing that listens before it speaks.

Emily Liao is a ghostwriter and memoirist with a background in law and AI. She came to the work after leaving a tech startup in 2021 and has since ghostwritten more than ten books with clients across four continents.

She specialises in business memoir: story-led, with hard-won lessons woven through the narrative. Her clients include CEOs, serial entrepreneurs, political refugees, and trauma survivors. Every project is different. The approach is the same: listen carefully, ask the uncomfortable question, write it well.
