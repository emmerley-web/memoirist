Who this is for

People with a story
worth telling

Ghostwriting suits people who have a story worth telling but not the time, the writing background, or the confidence to do it alone.

Clients include founders and executives who want their career on record, people writing a legacy memoir for family who will one day want to know where they came from, and survivors, artists, and advocates who have lived through something that deserves to be documented.

The common thread is not achievement or fame. It is having something genuine to say, and a real reason to say it.

About Emily

Writing that listens before it speaks.

Emily Liao is a ghostwriter and memoirist with a background in law and AI. She came to the work after leaving a tech startup in 2021 and has since ghostwritten more than ten books with clients across four continents.

She specialises in business memoir: story-led, with hard-won lessons woven through the narrative. Her clients include CEOs, serial entrepreneurs, political refugees, and trauma survivors. Every project is different. The approach is the same: listen carefully, ask the uncomfortable question, write it well.
