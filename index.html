The process

How it works

Every project follows the same structure, so you always know where things stand.

01

Free consultation

We discuss your story, goals, timeline, and whether we're the right fit for each other.

02

Interviews & drafting

Regular sessions to draw out your story. Between sessions, I write. You read and give feedback.

03

Revise & deliver

Revision rounds until it's right. When it's done, the manuscript is entirely yours.

About Emily

Writing that listens before it speaks.

Emily Liao is a ghostwriter and memoirist with a background in law and AI. She came to the work after leaving a tech startup in 2021 and has since ghostwritten more than ten books with clients across four continents.

She specialises in business memoir — story-led, with lessons woven throughout the narrative. Clients range from CEOs and serial entrepreneurs to political refugees and trauma survivors. Each project is different; the approach is the same: deep curiosity, active listening, and a lot of follow-up questions.
